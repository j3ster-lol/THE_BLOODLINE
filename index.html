WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN
A SERVER.

THE BLOODLINE is a private community where members come together, communicate and participate in shared activities.

Our goal is simple. Build a community where people know each other, communicate with each other and create their own place within the Bloodline.

Every member has their own position, role and place within the community. From regular members to the Family and leadership, everyone contributes to the identity of THE BLOODLINE.
